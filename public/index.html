Why a Health Passport?

We stopped treating health records like enterprise hospital silos. Instead, we give every human an indelible passport.

🛂

The Passport Metaphor

You own your passport. You carry it on your phone. When visiting a clinic or crossing borders, you present it for inspection. You cannot forge your own visas—only certified medical authorities stamp verified entries.

🔗

Git Append-Only Commits

Medical history is longitudinal. Past asthma or penicillin reactions are never overwritten. Every new lab, visit, or wearable summary is a cryptographically signed commit (Ed25519) referencing the parent block.

🩺

Doctor's 1-Page Summary

Doctors don't have 30 minutes to read 200 pages of scanned PDFs. In 30 seconds, the Copilot generates an instant mental model: Active Problems, Current Meds, Critical Allergies, and recent biomarkers.
