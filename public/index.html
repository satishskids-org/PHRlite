Frictionless Experience

How it works at your next doctor or school visit

Three simple steps. No app downloads required for your doctor.

1

1-Tap Consent Pass

At check-in, tap "Clinic Check-In" on your phone. Show the private QR code. This issues a temporary 4-hour read pass for that specific consultation.

2

Doctor Gets 30-Sec Clarity

Your doctor's computer immediately opens a clean, 1-page chronological summary with active prescriptions, critical allergies, and recent wearable trends.

3

Walk Out with an Official Stamp

At discharge, the clinician signs their prescription and encounter note. It is automatically stamped back into your sovereign passport before you leave the parking lot.
